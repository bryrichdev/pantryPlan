Get started

PantryPrep plans your week around what you already have. Five steps get you there.

A few steps are left before PantryPrep can build your grocery list.

  1. Add your ingredients (done)

    Everything you cook with. The fastest start is Add starter ingredients on the Ingredients page.

  2. Save a few recipes (done)

    Type in your own, or add the starter recipes on the Recipes page and edit them to taste.

  3. Stock your pantry (done)

    Enter what's in the pantry, fridge and freezer. Add expiry dates and this page tells you what to use first.

  4. Plan your week (done)

    Make a meal plan and put recipes on the days you'll cook them.

  5. Build your grocery list (done)

    PantryPrep lists what the plan needs, minus what you already have, sorted by aisle.
