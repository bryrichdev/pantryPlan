How it works

  1. Save what you cook

    Start from dozens of starter recipes or type in your own. Each one knows its ingredients.

  2. Plan the week

    Put recipes on days yourself, or let PantryPrep fill the week, using up what is about to expire first.

  3. Shop for what is missing

    The grocery list takes away what is already on your shelves and sorts the rest by aisle.

Cooking for a household? Make a group, share recipes with family and friends, and everyone copies what they like into their own kitchen.
